    Why these three

    Commercial hulls where they are solved

    The BlueBoat and BlueROV2 are known quantities with ArduPilot firmware, spares and a service history. Building our own would have spent the season on watertight enclosures instead of on cross-domain autonomy, which is what the 2026 rules reward.

    A custom aircraft where it matters

    Nothing off the shelf carries a delivery mechanism, floats after a ditching and stays under 7 kg. The 22 inch airframe is built in the lab so the payload mount, the flotation and the battery layout are ours to change.

    One interface behind all three

    Every vehicle speaks MAVLink to the same adapter. Swapping a simulated vehicle for a firmware build or a real hull is a line in a configuration file, which is how the same mission code has been exercised since before the hardware arrived.

    • BlueBoat, the surface vesselSafety chain, docking, firefighting, perception
    • BlueROV2, the underwater vehicleDepth hold autonomy, pipeline survey, magnetic repair
    • Quadrotor, the aircraftAirframe, failsafes, geofence, delivery mechanism
