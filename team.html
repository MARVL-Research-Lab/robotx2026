    The team

    MARVL is the Multi-Agent Robotics Vision and Learning Lab at the Singapore University of Technology and Design. RobotX 2026 is a lab-wide project across software, three vehicle builds and the safety case behind them.

    Who we are

    MARVL works on mobile robotics, multi-robot coordination, planning and scheduling, computer vision and machine learning, with field robotics on marine, ground and aerial platforms. The lab has run surface and underwater vehicles in Singapore waters for several years, and RobotX 2026 is the first time all three domains are flown together as one system.

    The competition entry is a student team under the RoboNation eligibility rules: at least three quarters of the members are full-time students, and students do the engineering. Staff and alumni advise.

    How the team is organised

    Work is split into four builds and three cross-cutting tracks. Everything meets at the ground station, which is the only piece of software that talks to all three vehicles and to RoboCommand.

    Vehicle builds

    One group per vehicle, each responsible for its own autopilot configuration, safety systems and proof of readiness package.

    Autonomy

    Mission orchestration, task state machines, the operator console and the simulation stack that all of it is tested against.

    Perception

    Buoy and pipeline detection, camera work on the ROV and the aircraft, and the datasets behind them.

    Safety and compliance

    Kill switch chains, failsafe behaviour, RF declarations, pilot licensing and the airworthiness case with CAAS.
